How to Use SHIELD BANK
📌 1. Customers

Each customer has an Account Number. Their Available Balance = Total Deposits – Total Withdrawals (only Cleared transactions).

Status meanings:

  • ✅ Active – Normal account.
  • ⏸️ Inactive – No activity for a while.
  • 💤 Dormant – Long‑term inactivity.
  • ⛔ Suspended – Temporarily blocked.
  • ❌ Closed – Account closed.
💰 2. Deposits & Withdrawals

Record deposits and withdrawals. The first deposit waives the opening fee. You cannot withdraw more than your available balance.

🏦 3. Loans

Apply, approve, disburse, complete, or reject loans. Click "Schedule" for amortization.

📊 4. Reports & PDF

Generate a weekly report and download as PDF. View customer statements with full transaction history.

🔍 5. Search

Use "Search by Account Number" to quickly find customers.

💡 All data is stored in the cloud (Supabase). You need an internet connection.
